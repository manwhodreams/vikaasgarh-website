ABOUT VIKAASGARH

Bringing Chhattisgarh’s Strengths Together

VikaasGarh is a sector-agnostic startup ecosystem-building initiative.

  • Supports business and agency owners in building, growing and scaling their businesses and agencies.
  • Enables collaboration and partnerships among startups, businesses and agencies for mutual growth.
  • Develops employable talent and enables employees to become intrapreneurs within their organizations.

VikaasGarh strengthens Chhattisgarh’s startup ecosystem through programs, events, networks, resources and initiatives developed in-house or undertaken through collaborations and partnerships with regional, national and global organizations. Through this work, it connects stakeholders across Chhattisgarh and builds links between the state’s startup ecosystem and wider ecosystems in India and globally.
